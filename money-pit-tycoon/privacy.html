Support

Stuck in a vault, lost progress or have an idea for the game? Write to us and include your device model and the vault number you are on.

enes3rkan@icloud.com
Where is my progress saved?
On your device. Deleting the app deletes your progress, and it does not move between devices.
I bought "Remove Ads" but still see ads.
Open the menu (top right) and tap Restore purchases. Optional bonus ads that you start yourself always stay available.
The game feels slow on my phone.
Open the menu and set Graphics to Low.
How do I find the vault key?
The key is in 3 pieces buried deep in the pile. Dig down to the floor; the Detector bonus shows the area of the nearest piece.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Money Pit Tycoon is a single-player mobile game. This policy explains what information is used when you play and why.

Information the game stores

Your progress (cash, vaults, upgrades and the manager name you type in) is saved only on your device. We do not run our own servers and do not receive this data.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as an advertising identifier, IP address, device model, general location and app interaction data. On iOS we ask for your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before your advertising identifier can be used for personalised ads; if you decline, you still see non-personalised ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time from Menu → Privacy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can reset your progress in the game menu, limit ad tracking in your device settings, and remove forced ads with the in-app purchase.

Contact

Questions about this policy: enes3rkan@icloud.com

Gizlilik Politikası

Son güncelleme: 28 Ekim 2026

Money Pit Tycoon tek oyunculu bir mobil oyundur. İlerlemen (para, kasalar, geliştirmeler, yazdığın isim) yalnızca cihazında saklanır; kendi sunucumuz yoktur ve bu verileri almayız.

Oyun, Google AdMob üzerinden reklam gösterir. Google, reklamları göstermek ve ölçmek için reklam kimliği, IP adresi, cihaz modeli, genel konum ve uygulama etkileşimi gibi bilgileri toplayabilir. iOS'ta reklam kimliğinin kişiselleştirilmiş reklam için kullanılmasından önce izin isteriz (App Tracking Transparency); izin vermezsen kişiselleştirilmemiş reklamlar görürsün. Avrupa Ekonomik Alanı'nda Google'ın onay formu gösterilir; tercihini Menü → Gizlilik bölümünden değiştirebilirsin.

"Reklamları kaldır" satın alımı tamamen App Store veya Google Play tarafından işlenir; ödeme bilgilerini görmeyiz. Oyun 13 yaşından küçük çocuklara yönelik değildir.
